Common questions

Things people ask before booking.

Will I get a certificate?

Always. Every notifiable job is certified and registered with building control through NICEIC, and you get the paperwork by email the same week.

Can you install an EV charger with a grant?

We are an OZEV approved installer, so if you qualify for a grant we handle the application as part of the job.

Do you do emergency work?

Yes, 24/7 for genuine electrical emergencies — burning smells, sparking, total power loss. Ring rather than email.
