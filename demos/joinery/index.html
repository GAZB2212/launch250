About us

One joiner. Twenty years. Every job start to finish.

Dave Kilbride served his time as a bench joiner and has been making fitted furniture on the Wirral since 2004. There is no sales team — the person who measures your room is the person who makes the piece and the person who fits it. That is why nothing ever gets lost in translation, and why the scribe lines actually fit your wonky Victorian walls.

Common questions

Things people ask before booking.

How long does a fitted wardrobe take?

Usually four to six weeks from design sign-off to fitting. Most of that is workshop time — the fit itself is normally one or two days.

Can you match existing woodwork?

Yes. We profile existing skirting and architrave and cut matching mouldings, which is why the work sits right in period houses.

Is the design visit really free?

Completely. We come out, measure, talk through options and send a fixed written quote. No deposit until you are happy with the drawings.
