Common questions

Things people ask before booking.

Do you charge a callout fee?

No. We quote a fixed price for the job before we start, and that is what you pay. If you decide not to go ahead, you owe us nothing.

How fast can you get to an emergency?

For genuine emergencies in the Wirral area we aim to be with you inside an hour, 24 hours a day. Ring us rather than using the form and you will speak to an engineer.

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes, and we will show you the card before we touch anything gas. Never let anyone work on your gas appliances without it.
